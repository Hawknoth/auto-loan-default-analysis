# Executive Summary

## Objective

Identify practical segments for early monitoring in an auto-loan portfolio using default, leverage, bureau score and recent delinquency.

## Key findings

- **233,154 loans** were analyzed; **21.7%** are marked as defaulted.
- The **80-90% LTV** band combines high volume with the highest observed default rate (**25.9%**).
- Borrowers with a delinquent account in the prior six months default at **27.1%**, compared with **21.3%** among borrowers without one.
- The **301-600 bureau-score** segment has the highest default rate (**27.1%**). Customers scoring 751-900 have the lowest (**14.7%**).
- State 13 has the highest default rate among states with at least 1,000 loans, at approximately **30.6%**.

## Recommended action

Use LTV, recent delinquency and bureau-score bands to create an early-monitoring queue. Geographic allocation should incorporate both account volume and default rate. Before operational deployment, validate the segments with monthly payment behavior, cure rate, roll rate and contact outcomes, which are not available in this dataset.
